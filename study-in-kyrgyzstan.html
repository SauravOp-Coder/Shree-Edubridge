Requirements

Admission requirements

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology
  • Minimum 50% marks for general category and NEET qualification for Indian students
  • Valid passport, medical reports, photographs, and other university-specific documents
  • Original documents may be required during visa and admission stages
