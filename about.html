Ask on WhatsApp
1. What is Shree Eduworld International?

Shree Eduworld International is an MBBS abroad consultancy helping students secure admissions in top medical universities in Russia, Kyrgyzstan, and Uzbekistan. We are “By the student, for the student” — fully transparent and student-focused.

2. Which countries do you offer MBBS programs in?

We provide MBBS admissions in Russia, Kyrgyzstan, and Uzbekistan.

3. What are the admission requirements?
  • Minimum 50% in PCB: Physics, Chemistry, Biology
  • NEET qualification is mandatory for Indian students
  • Valid passport
4. How much does it cost to study MBBS abroad?

The cost varies depending on the country and university. It generally includes tuition fees, hostel/accommodation, food, and living expenses. We maintain complete transparency with no hidden charges.

5. How long is the MBBS program?

The duration depends on the country. Kyrgyzstan generally follows 4.5 years academic plus 1 year internship, while Russia has 6 years MBBS including internship. Overall, MBBS duration is usually 5 to 6 years depending on the country.

6. Do you help with visa processing?

Yes, we provide complete visa assistance including documentation, application process support, and interview guidance.

7. Are scholarships available?

Some universities offer merit-based scholarships. We guide students based on eligibility and available options.

8. What about accommodation and hostel facilities?
  • Fully furnished hostels
  • Indian food availability in most universities
  • Safe and secure environment
9. Will my degree be recognized in my country?

Yes, the universities we recommend are recognized by WHO, NMC India, and other global medical bodies. Students must clear required licensing exams like FMGE/NEXT as applicable.

10. What are the job opportunities after MBBS abroad?

After completing MBBS, students can practice in their home country after clearing the required licensing exam, pursue PG abroad, or work internationally after clearing respective exams.

11. How do I apply through Shree Eduworld International?
  1. Contact us on WhatsApp
  2. Attend a free counselling session
  3. Choose your university
  4. Submit documents
  5. Start your admission process
12. What is your success rate?

We have a high success rate with successful student placements every year through proper guidance and transparency.

13. Is there any hidden charges?

No. We follow a 100% transparent policy with no hidden fees.

14. How can I contact Shree Eduworld International?

You can connect via WhatsApp, book a FREE 1-on-1 Zoom session, or tap the contact icons provided on the website.

15. Can I study other programs besides MBBS?

Yes, we also guide students for other international programs based on their interests and eligibility.
