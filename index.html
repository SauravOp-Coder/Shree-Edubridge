Admission process

Admission process in abroad

Studying abroad requires preparation and guidance to make a student an eligible candidate. We support the full process so that admission moves smoothly and effortlessly.

1

Application

After counselling and university selection, the team applies to the selected university using the scanned documents and student details.

2

Registration

Students pay the registration amount. After document approval from the university, registration in the admission process is completed.

3

Document submission

Students submit original documents for visa and admission letter processing, including marksheet, passport, HIV report, and photos.

4

Visa process

After receiving the invitation letter, the head office applies for the visa. The process generally takes 2 to 3 weeks depending on the country and embassy.

5

Travel arrangement

After visa approval, the team provides batch travel dates, books tickets, arranges departure, briefs students, and receives them at the airport abroad.

6

Contract signature

After arriving at the university, students sign the education contract, get hostel allotment, and receive pass cards and ID cards.

+

On-ground support

Our team stays present for smoother hostel allotment, identity cards, and other important first-week procedures.
