Admission requirements

Documents and eligibility

  • 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry and Biology.
  • NEET qualification mandatory for Indian students.
  • Valid passport, 12th marksheet, HIV test report and passport-size photos.
  • University-specific documents and original document submission during visa/admission stages.
Process

Admission process

1

Application

Apply to the selected university after counselling using scanned documents and student details.

2

Registration

Pay registration amount and complete university registration after document approval.

3

Visa process

Apply for visa after invitation letter; the process generally takes 2–3 weeks depending on country and embassy.

4

Travel & arrival

Ticket booking, departure briefing, airport receiving, hostel allotment and ID/pass card support.
